## Phase 2: Research - Completed

### Key Findings

#### 1. Office Ergonomics & Shoulder Pain
- **Prevalence:** 13-24% of shoulder pain complaints are work-related
- **Primary Risk Factors:**
  - Prolonged static posture (shoulder flexion, reaching)
  - Improper desk height and keyboard position
  - Frequent mouse use with extended reach
  - Poor workstation ergonomics
- **Corrective Actions:**
  - Move keyboard/mouse closer to body
  - Adjust chair height for neutral shoulder position
  - Take 5-minute breaks per hour of intensive computer work
  - Use headset for phone calls to avoid shoulder elevation

#### 2. Upper Crossed Syndrome (UCS)
- **Definition:** Common postural distortion pattern involving muscle imbalances
- **Muscle Pattern:**
  - **Tight/Overactive:** Upper trapezius, levator scapulae, pectoralis major/minor
  - **Weak/Inhibited:** Deep neck flexors, middle/lower trapezius, rhomboids
- **Symptoms:**
  - Rounded shoulders and forward head posture
  - Chronic neck and shoulder discomfort
  - Stiffness and tension headaches
- **Treatment:**
  - Stretch tight muscles (pectorals, upper traps, levator scapulae)
  - Strengthen weak muscles (deep neck flexors, mid/lower traps, rhomboids)
  - Postural re-education

#### 3. Chronic Shoulder Pain Guidelines
- **Initial Evaluation:** History, red flags assessment, clinical examination
- **Imaging:** X-rays first; MRI not recommended for early shoulder pain
- **Conservative Treatment:**
  - Acetaminophen for pain relief
  - NSAIDs for inflammation
  - Rehabilitation exercises (recommended)
- **Rotator Cuff Tendinopathy:**
  - Exercise therapy is first-line treatment
  - Progressive loading and strengthening
  - Scapular stabilization exercises

#### 4. Exercises for Computer Users
- **Recommended Exercises:**
  - Side neck release (sternocleidomastoid stretch)
  - Scapular retraction exercises
  - Chin tucks (deep neck flexor strengthening)
  - Doorway chest stretches
  - Shoulder shrugs with proper form
- **Frequency:** 2-3 sets, 10-15 reps daily
- **Duration:** Hold stretches 30 seconds
